07 · iOS finance app · Redesign

An iOS app from 2017, brought to 2026.

RoboLedger tracks what a robotics team spends and earns over a season, so it can report to sponsors and grant bodies. The app worked, but it was built to old iOS patterns, and in places it got the money wrong.

Role
Sole product designer: UX, UI, system
Product
iOS finance tracker for robotics teams
Scope
20 screens · every state
System
40 tokens · 12 text styles · 12 components
Platform
iOS · 393 × 852

The case in 30 seconds

The problem
The expenses screen filtered by income categories, totals read $0 above a full list, a one-of-five choice was drawn as checkboxes, and red and green were the only way to tell income from spending.
What I did
Rebuilt it to current iOS: entry in a sheet with a detent, a labelled tab bar, every state of the core screen, a two-step onboarding and an honest paywall, all built from one tokenised library.
The result
A ship-ready file a developer can build from: 20 screens made entirely of component instances, with a Reduce Transparency fallback built into the bars.
Read the full story ↓

01 — The app

Twenty screens, every state, one library: sheets with detents, a labelled tab bar and accessibility built into the system rather than added afterwards.

02 — Every state, not just the happy one

Empty, no limit, within limit, over limit: the same components telling four different truths.

03 — Amount first, category required

The amount takes focus at once and the keypad is the whole lower half. A one-of-five choice now reads as one of five, and every expense needs a category, because it has to be reportable.

04 — Numbers a sponsor can read

Every figure is an amount, not just a share. Red and green are retired: they are the pair colour-vision deficiency affects most, so every row carries a label and an amount instead.

05 — Two steps, then an honest paywall

Onboarding asks for a team name and a limit, nothing else. The paywall shows both plans, prices the annual one per month, and closes with one tap.

06 — A library a developer can build from

16 colour tokens and 24 spacing, radius and size tokens, 12 text styles and 12 components with variants. Every screen is made of instances.

In short

The same app, now telling the truth about the money.

Platform fluency was the point: current iOS patterns where they help, and the numbers a sponsor reads made impossible to misread.
